The VISHŌ keyword tool

The VISHŌ keyword tool is an internal tool used only by the VISHŌ team. It connects to the Google Ads account of VISHŌ with that account's permission.

What Google data the tool uses

Only keyword planning data from the Google Ads API: keyword ideas, average monthly searches and competition level. It does not read email, files, contacts or any other Google account data.

How we use it

To research which search terms people use in Belgium and the Netherlands, for the VISHŌ website and for the websites of our clients.

Storage

Results are saved as files on VISHŌ's own computers and VISHŌ's own Google Drive. The access key for the Google account stays on VISHŌ's computer and is never shared.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data. We do not use it for advertising or to train AI models. Keyword volumes can appear in reports we make for our clients.

Google API Services User Data Policy

VISHŌ's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask us to delete data, email brahim@visho.studio.
